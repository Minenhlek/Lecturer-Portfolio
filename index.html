I am a passionate Junior Lecturer at Belgium Campus iTversity, dedicated to making programming and IT accessible to every student. My journey in technology started with a deep curiosity for how software shapes the world — and that curiosity drives my teaching every day.

At Belgium Campus, I focus on creating an engaging, student-centred learning environment where complex concepts become clear through hands-on practice, real-world examples, and collaborative problem solving.

I hold a degree in Information Technology and continue to grow professionally through research, industry engagement, and continuous learning in emerging technologies.
